Our philosophy

We build long-term care around the whole person.

Every person has different needs, preferences, and routines. Elmwood Cottage begins by listening, then creates a practical plan for dependable care over time.

Families receive clear communication and care that can adapt as needs change. The goal is steady support, familiar routines, and dignity in every interaction.

Getting started, step by step

A clear path to ongoing care.

01 · Conversation

Tell us about the person, their routines, ongoing needs, and the support your family is looking for.

02 · Assessment

Low-dose 3D scan, digital intra-oral scan, and a smile-line photo set. All completed in under 22 minutes.

03 · Care plan

Together we define the skilled, non-skilled, or combined support that fits the person and family.

04 · Funding review

We review private pay or private insurance arrangements before services begin. Coverage varies by policy.

05 · Care begins

Care begins with clear expectations, familiar routines, and communication with the people who matter.

06 · Ongoing review

We revisit the plan as needs change so long-term support remains appropriate and personal.

Questions, answered

What families want to know first.

If you are unsure which level of care is right, start with a conversation. We will listen and explain the next practical step.

Ask a question
Does Elmwood Cottage provide short-term care?
Elmwood Cottage focuses only on long-term care. We build ongoing care relationships rather than temporary arrangements.
What is the difference between skilled and non-skilled care?
Skilled care addresses clinical needs requiring trained oversight. Non-skilled care supports daily activities such as personal care, meals, mobility, and companionship.
How can care be paid for?
Elmwood Cottage accepts private pay and private insurance. Coverage, authorization, deductibles, and out-of-pocket costs vary by policy.
Can a plan include both types of care?
Plan for 75 minutes: 25 minutes of conversation, 22 minutes of diagnostics, and 32 minutes to walk through your tailored plan.
